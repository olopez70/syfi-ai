Usage Guidelines

Primary Brand Identity
Use Radar Blue as your primary brand color for logos, main CTAs, and key brand elements. It conveys reliability and technological innovation.
Technology & Trust
The blue family creates a sense of trust and technological sophistication, perfect for tech companies, healthcare, and professional services.
Calming Interface
Radar Blue variations create a calming, professional interface that reduces eye strain and promotes focus in applications.
Warm Contrast
Use the beige/sand complementary colors sparingly for warm contrast and to highlight important secondary elements.
Grey Hierarchy System
Use the 8-shade grey system to create clear visual hierarchy. Light greys for backgrounds, medium greys for secondary text, dark greys for primary content.
Cool Grey Harmony
These greys have subtle blue undertones that harmonize perfectly with Radar Blue, creating a cohesive, professional appearance throughout your interface.
Accessibility First
All color combinations meet WCAG AA standards. The Deep Navy provides excellent contrast for text readability.
Yellow for Energy & Focus
Use Professional Yellow sparingly for important calls-to-action, highlights, and elements that need immediate attention. It creates perfect contrast with Radar Blue.
Blue-Yellow Balance
Follow the 70-20-10 rule: 70% blues and neutrals, 20% complementary colors (beige/sand), 10% yellow accents for maximum impact and readability.
Sky & Weather Theme
The blue-yellow combination naturally evokes sky, sun, and weather themes - perfect for weather apps, aviation, or environmental brands.
